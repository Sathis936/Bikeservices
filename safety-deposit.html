Security Deposit & Safety Information

We believe in 100% transparency with zero surprise deductions.

What is the Deposit?

A pre-authorization hold on your card (₹120 to ₹250 depending on model) to guarantee vehicle return in good condition.

Instant Release Timeline

Upon returning the scooter with fuel level matching pickup and no damages, the hold is released within 24 hours.

Damage Waiver Tiers

Opt for our optional ₹10/day Comprehensive Damage Waiver to cap your maximum liability at ₹50 in case of minor scratches.

Rider Safety Checklist

Always Wear Helmet

Helmets are required by law for both the rider and pillion passenger. We supply cleaned DOT-approved helmets with every rental.

Observe Speed Limits

Maintain safe riding speeds, especially on cobblestone streets, tram tracks, and wet asphalt during rain.
