Frequently Asked Questions

For scooters with a top speed under 45 km/h, a standard valid car driver's license is sufficient. For our high-speed highway-capable models (such as the PCX 160 or Super Soco CPx), a standard motorcycle endorsement is required.

You can recharge at any of our 18 fast-charging swap stations for free, or plug the provided portable charging adapter into any standard 110V/220V wall socket at your home or hotel.

A pre-authorization hold (₹120 to ₹250 depending on model) is placed on your credit/debit card at check-in. Once you return the vehicle in good condition, the hold is released immediately within 24 hours.
