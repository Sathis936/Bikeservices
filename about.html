Our Journey & Mission

Empowering Urban Riders Since 2018

Rent o Ride was founded with a single mission: to replace congested traffic and expensive car hires with enjoyable, agile, and eco-friendly two-wheeled mobility.

What started with 10 classic Vespas in a single city garage has grown into an international fleet of over 350+ smart electric and fuel-efficient maxi scooters across 18 stations.

Our Mission

Provide affordable, high-quality, frictionless micromobility for tourists and daily commuters alike.

Our Vision

Transform city landscapes into 100% zero-emission micro-transport hubs by 2030.

Why Riders Choose Us

Engineered for Freedom & Safety
